# How to navigate your Het Ritme planner

Nine screens, grouped into three phases. The rail down the left side is numbered, so there is always
one obvious next step.

---

## Phase 1 — Start (once)

### 1. Set up
Your currency, your year, your start month, how you like to budget, your income, your categories.
Open it once. Come back only when you want to add a category.

*Budget method:* **Zero-based** gives every unit of money a job each month and starts fresh.
**Carry-over** rolls what is left into next month. If you are unsure, start with carry-over — it is
more forgiving.

### 2. Accounts
Where money actually sits. Enter each account and today's balance. Record transfers between accounts
here — including credit-card payments, which are transfers, not new spending.

---

## Phase 2 — Every week (five minutes)

### 3. Add spending
The screen you will use most. Two tables:
- **Fixed bills** — entered once, repeat all year. Tick them off as they are paid.
- **Everything else** — date, amount, category. Newest row sits at the top, so there is no scrolling.

You can log a whole week in under a minute. That is the point.

### 4. This month
Where you find out whether you are okay. Three numbers at the top: what is left, what you have spent,
what you have saved. The category table underneath shows planned against actual. At the bottom,
**one thing to do next** — a single suggestion, which you are free to ignore.

Use the month switcher at the top to move between months. All twelve live behind it.

### 5. Bills calendar
The shape of your month. Bills laid out on a calendar, with a weekly summary down the side. Useful
before payday, and it prints one month to a page.

---

## Phase 3 — The big picture (whenever you want it)

Nothing in this phase is required. The weekly loop works without ever opening these.

### 6. Year overview
Every month against every category. Toggle between budgeted and actual. Optional panels inside:
the 50/30/20 check, who spends what, and a no-spend challenge.

### 7. Goals & savings
Sinking funds — a holiday, a new laptop, an emergency buffer. Set a target, log what you save, watch
the bar fill.

### 8. Debt
Your debts, a strategy, and a payoff date that recalculates every time you pay something.
**Snowball** clears the smallest balance first (faster wins). **Avalanche** clears the highest interest
first (less interest paid). The screen shows both dates before you choose.

### 9. Net worth
What you own minus what you owe, tracked month to month. The slowest-moving screen and the most
satisfying one after a year.

---

## Two editions, same nine screens

If you bought **Calm**, it is deliberately quiet: soft paper tones, one accent colour, no celebration
graphics. A finished goal simply reads *goal met*.

If you bought **Loud**, the same nine screens come with badges, a streak counter and celebration
moments when a goal lands.

Same logic, same numbers. If you ever want the other one, the layout is identical — you will not have
to relearn anything.
