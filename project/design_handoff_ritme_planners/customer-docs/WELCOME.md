# Welcome to your Het Ritme Budget

*Planning is a rhythm, not a rule.*

You do not need to read this whole file to start. Four things, about four minutes, and you are set up.

---

## Four things, then you are done setting up

**1. Pick your currency and start month**
Two cells on the **Set up** screen. Everything else follows from these.

**2. Name your income and your categories**
Ten categories is plenty. You can add more in month three, when you know better.

**3. Enter your account balances**
Today's numbers, not perfect ones. That is the **Accounts** screen.

**4. Add your fixed bills once**
Rent, phone, subscriptions. They repeat themselves for the rest of the year after this.

Then: **five minutes, once a week.** Add what you spent, look at **This month**, close the file.

---

## The only thing to remember

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| A **white** cell | Yours. Type here. |
| A **grey** cell | Works itself out. Leave it alone. |
| A header with a **\*** | Needed for the maths to work |

That is the whole instruction manual. The legend sits on every screen, so you never have to remember it.

---

## If something looks wrong

- **A total looks empty.** A required (\*) field is probably blank. The screen will name it.
- **You typed in a grey cell and got a warning.** Nothing is broken — click Cancel. Grey cells
  calculate themselves.
- **You want to move data.** Copy and paste rather than cut and paste. Cutting can move a formula's
  reference with it.
- **A month looks empty.** Check the month switcher at the top of **This month**.

---

## Missing a week is fine

Nothing in this planner resets your progress. Streaks keep your best run. A missed week is data, not
failure. Come back on any day of any month and the numbers will still make sense.
