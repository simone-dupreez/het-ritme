# HET RITME — brand

## Name
**Het Ritme** — Dutch for *the rhythm*. Chosen by the owner over the alternate candidates (Ordo, Doable).

- Pronounced "het RIT-muh". English speakers arrive at the meaning phonetically.
- The definite article does real work: it makes the name a **thing you have** rather than an abstract
  quality. *Your* rhythm, named. It also reads unmistakably Dutch, which grounds the
  European-born story without excluding anyone.
- Not ADHD-coded, so the brand does not age out or exclude non-ADHD customers.
- Owner is Dutch/South African; the name reads cleanly in Dutch, English and Afrikaans.

### Written forms
| Context | Form |
|---|---|
| Wordmark, headings, shop name | `HET RITME` (uppercase) |
| Running prose, descriptions, emails | Het Ritme |
| Filenames, handles, slugs | `het-ritme`, `@hetritme`, hetritme.shop |
| Second reference in body copy | "Ritme" alone is acceptable once the full name has appeared |

Never "HetRitme", "het ritme" lowercase in a heading, or "The Rhythm" translated.

### Wordmark
**Bricolage Grotesque 600**, uppercase, two words on one line.

- `HET RITME` at `letter-spacing: .2em` — reduced from the single-word `.28em` so the two-word
  lockup does not sprawl. Word space is a single space, not an em space.
- **Compact variant** for narrow frames (favicons, thumbnails, planner corners): `RITME` alone at
  `.28em`, with the mark carrying the identity. Use the full name at least once per surface.
- **Stacked variant**: `HET` on the first line at 55% of the size and `.32em`, `RITME` beneath at full
  size and `.2em`, both left-aligned or both centred — never mixed.

## Mark — the Focus Ring
A ring with a centred dot: attention landing on one thing.

- Ring: circle, stroke = 1/10 of diameter, color = ink `#262119`
- Dot: centred circle, diameter = 1/4 of ring diameter, color = the edition accent
  (Calm `#4A6250`, Loud `#D65A34`)
- Lockup: mark + `HET RITME` on one horizontal line, gap = 0.45 x mark diameter
- Stacked lockup (mark above the stacked wordmark) for square frames: avatars, Notion page icon,
  planner covers
- **Below 20px, drop the wordmark and use the mark alone.** Never shrink the wordmark into illegibility.
- Between 20px and 40px, use the compact `RITME` wordmark rather than squeezing both words.
- Reversed: ring and dot both in paper `#F6F2E9` on ink or on an accent field

## Voice
**Calm voice** — plain, respectful, quietly confident. Behavioural-economics framing without jargon.
No exclamation marks. No emoji. Never cute.
> "Enter last month's actuals before you set this month's targets. Working from real numbers rather
> than intentions is the whole trick."

**Loud voice** — warm, funny, self-aware, never manic. Humour aimed at the situation, never at the user.
Max one exclamation mark per screen. No emoji in headings.
> "Fun money went €41 over. Honestly? Fine."
> "Savings goal: done. Go be a person."

**Both voices always:**
- Frame missed weeks as data, never as failure
- Say what to do next, in one sentence
- Never say "just", "simply", or "all you have to do"
- Never imply the user lacks discipline

## Do not
- Do not use handwriting fonts, hand-drawn doodles, or emoji as UI
- Do not use uppercase micro-labels as the primary labelling system (rejected in review)
- Do not put the Loud palette on the Calm edition or vice versa
- Do not copy any competitor's graphics, layouts, or listing copy
