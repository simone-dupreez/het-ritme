# Het Ritme — SEO & marketing playbook

**Shop name:** Het Ritme · **Etsy shop slug:** `HetRitme` · **handle:** @hetritme · **domain:** hetritme.shop

Note on the name in search: "Het Ritme" is a brand term with no search volume yet, so it never carries a
listing title's front 40 characters. Keywords go first, the brand goes in the shop name, the banner, and
the description sign-off. Build brand recognition through Pinterest and email, not through Etsy titles.

## 1. Etsy SEO fundamentals

Etsy ranks on **title + tags + attributes + recency + conversion**. Tags matter most; titles should
front-load the phrase a buyer actually types.

### Title formula
`<primary keyword> <format> — <edition/differentiator>, <platforms>, <qualifier>`

Front 40 characters carry the most weight — put the primary keyword there.

**Live titles**

- Minimalist Budget, Calm:
  `ADHD Budget Planner Spreadsheet & Notion Template — Minimalist Low Stimulation Annual Budget, Excel Google Sheets Numbers, Any Currency`
- Dopamine Budget, Loud:
  `Dopamine Budget Planner for ADHD — Reward Based Annual Budget Spreadsheet & Notion Template with Badges and Streaks, Excel Google Sheets`
- Debt Buster, Calm:
  `Debt Payoff Planner Spreadsheet & Notion Template — Snowball & Avalanche Debt Tracker for ADHD, Excel Google Sheets Numbers`
- Single Girl Meal Planner:
  `Meal Planner for One — Flexible Weekly Meal Plan & Shopping List Spreadsheet + Notion Template, ADHD Friendly, No Pressure`

### Tags (13 per listing, all 20 characters or under)
Core set, reused with 3–4 swapped per SKU:
`adhd planner` · `budget spreadsheet` · `notion budget` · `annual budget` · `google sheets` ·
`excel template` · `neurodivergent` · `minimalist planner` · `money tracker` · `paycheck budget` ·
`sinking funds` · `debt payoff` · `savings tracker`

Per-SKU swaps:
- Dopamine/Loud → `dopamine planner`, `habit tracker`, `reward tracker`, `adhd budget`
- Debt → `debt snowball`, `debt free`, `loan tracker`
- Meal → `meal planner`, `meal prep`, `grocery list`, `cooking for one`
- Cleaning → `cleaning schedule`, `chore chart`, `home planner`

**Rules:** never repeat a word across more than 4 tags; use multi-word phrases (single words rarely
convert); no misspellings; no competitor brand names.

### Attributes & category
Category: *Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Calendars & Planners* (digital). Set: Digital download,
Instant download, File type, Language. Fill every attribute — Etsy uses them for filtering.

### Keyword targets, by intent
| Intent | Phrase | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Condition-led | adhd budget planner, adhd friendly spreadsheet | Titles, tags, description opener |
| Platform-led | notion budget template, google sheets budget | Titles, tags |
| Method-led | zero based budget, debt snowball, sinking funds | Description, section headers |
| Aesthetic-led | minimalist budget planner, low stimulation planner | Calm titles, tags |
| Reward-led | dopamine planner, gamified budget | Loud titles, tags |

## 2. Listing anatomy that converts

**Image order** (the single biggest conversion lever — image 1 is 80% of the click decision):
1. Hero — product name, one promise, a real-looking file
2. The nine-screen map ("nine screens, not twenty-nine tabs")
3. The three cell states ("there are only three kinds of cell")
4. A real screenshot of *This month*
5. Formats + currency
6. *(add later)* a 15-second screen recording as the video slot

**Description structure**
1. One sentence naming the exact problem ("twenty-nine tabs and no idea which one to open")
2. Setup promise: four minutes, then five minutes a week
3. What you get — the nine screens listed by name
4. Both formats included
5. Any currency, any date format
6. The other edition exists (cross-sell inside every listing)
7. Instant download, free lifetime updates, message me

Full approved copy for both editions is in the design canvas, artboard **3d**.

**Pricing ladder** (digital planners, EU market)
- Single planner, one edition, both formats: **€14–19**
- Both editions of one planner: **€24** (framed as "+€5 for the other edition")
- Budget bundle (3 budget planners, both editions): **€39**
- Full shop bundle (all 6, both editions): **€59**
Launch: 20% off first two weeks; never discount below €12 (it reads as low quality in this category).

## 4. Launch plan (first 60 days)

**Weeks 1–2 — foundation**
- Publish 4 listings: Minimalist Budget and Dopamine Budget, both formats (each listing carries both
  formats; edition is the SKU split)
- Free lead magnet: a one-month single-page version of This month, in both editions, in exchange for an
  email. This is what builds the list.
- 10 Pinterest pins per listing (Pinterest drives the majority of digital-planner traffic)

**Weeks 3–4 — proof**
- Ship Debt Buster + Single Girl Meal Planner
- Ask the first 20 buyers for reviews with a note in the download folder (never in-app nagging)
- Start a "how I built this" thread on Reddit r/ADHD and r/adhdwomen — participate honestly, do not
  drop links cold

**Weeks 5–8 — compounding**
- Ship remaining two planners, complete the 24-SKU grid
- Bundle listings live
- First email sequence to the lead-magnet list (see below)
- Reach out to 10 mid-size ADHD creators with free copies of both editions — the two-edition story is
  itself the pitch

## 5. Channel notes

**Pinterest** (primary traffic)
- 1000 × 1500 pins, text overlay readable at thumbnail size
- One pin per angle, not per product: "nine screens not twenty-nine tabs", "the budget you'll still be
  using in June", "two editions, same planner", "how to budget when your brain won't"
- 3–5 fresh pins a day at launch; repin evergreen monthly
- Board names as keyword real estate: *ADHD budgeting*, *Notion budget templates*, *Minimalist money*

**Instagram / TikTok**
- Show the file, not your face, if you prefer. Screen recordings of the weekly five-minute loop.
- Highest-performing format in this niche: the side-by-side — same screen, Calm vs. Loud, no voiceover
- Hooks that fit the brand voice: *"Twenty-nine tabs is why you quit budgeting."* /
  *"Same planner. Two brains."*

**Email** (5-part welcome sequence after the lead magnet)
1. Here is your free month + the four-minute setup
2. The three cell states — why most templates fail on instruction, not features
3. Zero-based vs. carry-over, explained without jargon
4. Calm or Loud? A short honest guide to which edition suits you
5. One-time 15% code, 48 hours

**Reviews & retention**
- Ship a small *what's new in v1.1* note to past buyers — free lifetime updates are a genuine
  differentiator, so say so
- Answer every message within 24h; response rate feeds Etsy's star seller metrics

## 6. Measure only these
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Listing views → favourites | > 4% |
| Views → orders | > 1.5% (digital planners) |
| Pinterest outbound clicks / week | 200 by week 8 |
| Email list | 500 by day 60 |
| Reviews | 20 by day 60 |

Ignore impressions. They move with Etsy's mood, not your work.

## 7. Words to avoid in all copy
"just", "simply", "all you have to do", "hack your brain", "fix your ADHD", "productivity porn",
"cure", "discipline". Anything implying the customer is the broken part.
