## Description

Four nights planned, three nights free. That is not failing at meal prep — that is winning.

This meal planner is built for people who actually live alone and cooks some nights and absolutely does not cook other nights. Plan your four dinners. Get the shopping list. Go once, come home. The three nights you're not cooking? That is not a failure state. That is strategy.

**What you get**
A weekly meal planner sized for actual solo living. Four dinner slots. Notes for each (leftovers on purpose, going out, order in — your call). The shopping list updates itself from whatever you planned, nothing more. A Notion version lets you duplicate it into your own workspace and build your recipe library over time.

**Both formats included**
A spreadsheet for Excel, Google Sheets and Apple Numbers, with live formulas. Plus a Notion version you duplicate into your own workspace.

**Built by someone who gets it**
The tone assumes four nights is reasonable and three nights off is a feature. No guilt about takeout. No assumption that you enjoy cooking as a hobby. Every week you finish this, you get a streak.

**If you prefer minimalist planning**
The same planner exists as Meal Planner, the Calm edition, with the same four-nights structure and none of the celebration bells.

Instant download. Free lifetime updates. Message me if anything is unclear — I answer every one.
