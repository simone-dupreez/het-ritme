## Description

Most budget templates fail for the same reason: twenty-nine tabs and no idea which one to open. This one has nine screens, grouped into three phases, so there is always one obvious next step.

Set it up once in about four minutes: your currency, your categories, your balances, your fixed bills. After that it is five minutes a week — add what you spent, look at one screen, close the file.

**What you get**
Nine screens: Set up, Accounts, Add spending, This month, Bills calendar, Year overview, Goals & savings, Debt, Net worth. Twelve months behind one switcher. Zero-based or carry-over budgeting. Recurring bills entered once. Sinking funds, debt payoff and net worth tracking included.

**Both formats included**
A spreadsheet for Excel, Google Sheets and Apple Numbers, with live formulas and protected cells. Plus a Notion version you duplicate into your own workspace — same nine screens, same logic.

**Any currency, any date format**
One cell sets your symbol everywhere. European and US number and date formats both included. Designed in Europe, built for anyone.

**Designed for low stimulation**
Soft paper tones, one accent colour, generous space, no flashing, no clutter. If you would rather be cheered on, the same planner exists as the Dopamine edition with badges, streaks and celebration moments.

Instant download. Free lifetime updates. Message me if anything is unclear — I answer every one.
